# Reconciliation and refunds

POL-RECON-01 Reconciliation. Every incoming bank payment is matched against completed settlements. Payments that cannot be matched become reconciliation exceptions and are reviewed by finance operations.

POL-WRITEOFF-01 Small differences. A payment that is short by at most 5.00 EUR against one open settlement may be matched with a write-off. Larger differences require remittance advice from the payer.

POL-REFUND-01 Duplicate payments. A payment received twice is refunded to the payer once reconciliation has confirmed the duplicate. Refunds are never issued for payments that are only unmatched.
