# Payment terms

POL-TERMS-01 Standard payment terms. Every order is invoiced on the order date and is payable within 30 days (net 30). The payment is collected as a settlement from the customer's payer account to the merchant receivable account ACC-3000 when the order is placed.

POL-TERMS-02 Early payment discount. Customers who pay within 10 days receive a 2 % discount on the invoice amount. The discount is granted as a credit note and never by changing the settled amount.

POL-TERMS-03 Currency. All orders of the acme tenant are priced, invoiced and settled in EUR. Orders in other currencies are not accepted.
